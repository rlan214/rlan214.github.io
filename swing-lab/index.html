    Foam-ball tracker (beta)

    Rough start line (down-the-line) or launch angle and speed (face-on) from the first frames after impact. Phone cameras are too slow for spin or carry. A white or bright ball against a darker background works best.

    Setup

    1. Tripod at hip height, 8–10 ft away, phone upright (portrait).
    2. Whole body in frame with room above your head and below your feet.
    3. Good light. Avoid a bright window behind you.
    4. Set up at address and hold still about 1 second. A beep means it's armed. Then make a full swing.
    5. Keep the phone in the same spot every session so results compare fairly.
