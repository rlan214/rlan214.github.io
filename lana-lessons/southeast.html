Notes for Dad (not for the test)

The practice uses the worksheet's answers word for word. These lines on the sheet are wrong or oversimplified:

  • Cotton gin: it pulled the seeds out of raw cotton. Spinning and weaving are what turn cotton into cloth.
  • Kennedy Space Center: it's NASA's main launch site. NASA's research happens at many centers (Houston, Langley, JPL and others).
  • Mardi Gras: it's the last party day before Lent begins, about 6–7 weeks before Easter, not right before Easter.
  • Kitty Hawk: the 1903 flights and the memorial are in Kill Devil Hills, the town next to Kitty Hawk. Usually spelled "Kitty Hawk."
  • "Mexican" food influence: Southeast cooking is usually credited to African, French, Spanish, Caribbean and Native American influences.
  • World of Coca-Cola: it's a museum with a small bottling exhibit, not the largest Coca-Cola factory.
  • Tides make swamps: mostly flat land, rivers and rain make the swamps. The Everglades is fed by fresh water flowing south from Lake Okeechobee.
