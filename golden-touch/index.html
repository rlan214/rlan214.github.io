How the daily session works

Every day follows the same pattern youth coaches use for at-home ball mastery: a warm-up with toe taps, then this week's skill drills (30 seconds of work, short rest, both feet), the Box Challenge (chase the targets and change direction), and a quick stretch. Each skill focus lasts 7 sessions, then moves on: Foot Familiarity → Directional Control → Change of Direction → Quick Feet & Moves. After 4 weeks it starts over with harder targets. Missed a day? No restart, just pick up where you left off.
